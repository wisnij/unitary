Unitary is a unit converter and calculator that understands units. Type "5 ft + 3 in" and convert it to centimeters, or "3e4 kilometers/week" to miles per hour. Units are carried through every calculation, so a mix-up such as adding meters to seconds is caught instead of quietly giving a wrong answer.

No ads, trackers, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Unitary collects no personal information, and everything works offline.

FREEFORM CALCULATOR
• Arithmetic, powers, fractions, and parentheses, with units anywhere: "sqrt(9 m^2) + sin(45 degrees) * 5 ft"
• Full dimensional analysis: joules convert to kilowatt-hours, and incompatible units are an error
• Temperature conversions such as tempF(212) to tempC
• Mathematical and trigonometric functions, SI prefixes, and physical constants such as c, h, and pi
• Suggestions for unit, prefix, and function names as you type
• A history of past conversions, recalled with one tap
• Enter a unit's name on its own to see how it is defined
• Expression syntax compatible with GNU Units

WORKSHEETS
Type a value into any row and see it in every other unit at once. Twelve worksheets are built in: Angle, Area, Currency, Digital Storage, Energy, Length, Mass, Pressure, Speed, Temperature, Time, and Volume.

UNIT BROWSER
Browse over 7,000 units, 125 prefixes, and more than 100 functions, alphabetically or grouped by physical dimension, with search and a detail page for each one.

CURRENCY
Exchange rates for dozens of currencies, plus gold, silver, and platinum. Current rates are fetched automatically when the stored rates are more than a day old, or whenever you refresh them, and built-in rates mean currency conversion works offline too.

DESIGNED FOR DAILY USE
• Light and dark themes, following your system setting
• Phone and tablet layouts, in portrait and landscape
• Screen reader support and high-contrast colors
• Adjustable precision and number notation
